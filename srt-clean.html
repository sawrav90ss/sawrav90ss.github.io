srt-clean: free Mac cleaner and Windows PC cleaner

Free up disk space with one command, without losing anything you need. Nothing to install.

View on GitHub

How to use it

Mac

Open Terminal, paste this line and press Enter:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sawrav90ss/srt-clean/main/srt-clean.sh | bash

Windows

Open PowerShell (press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter), paste this line and press Enter:

irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sawrav90ss/srt-clean/main/srt-clean.ps1 | iex

srt-clean shows you what it found and how big it is, then asks Delete these? (y/n). Type y to clean or n to leave everything as it is.

What it cleans

  • Cache files of every app: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Discord, Slack, VS Code, Adobe and more
  • Temp and junk files, logs, crash reports
  • Video editing render caches: Premiere Pro, After Effects, Final Cut Pro, DaVinci Resolve
  • Graphics shader caches (NVIDIA, AMD, DirectX) on Windows
  • As administrator on Windows: Windows temp files and Windows Update leftovers

What it never touches

  • Your documents, photos, videos, music and projects
  • Passwords, logins, cookies, bookmarks and browser history
  • Apps that are running right now
  • The Trash and the Recycle Bin

Why use it

srt-clean is a simple, free and open source alternative to paid cleaner apps. It only removes files that apps rebuild on their own, and it always asks first.
